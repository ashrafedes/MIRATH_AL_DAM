## Character sheets required
For every recurring character, maintain: approved front/three-quarter/profile/back views; neutral and key expressions; exact hair/facial hair; body proportions; wardrobe IDs; voice profile; relationship map; story objective, fear, secret, arc; scene-specific changes; approval status.

## Story canon not inferred from a visual board
The board’s biographical text is a visual-development draft unless separately confirmed by the locked script. Confirm ages, relationships, occupations, secrets, and character arcs against the owner-approved story before locking them.
