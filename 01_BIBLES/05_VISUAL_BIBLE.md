# Visual Bible — ميراث الدم

**Version:** v0.1  
**Status:** Initial look direction based on the owner-approved reference boards.

## Look and feel
- Cinematic, grounded Egyptian drama.
- Restrained, dramatic contrast; realistic skin and materials.
- Dark burgundy, charcoal/black, warm cream, muted taupe, and restrained gold.
- Warm practical interior light balanced with cooler shadow; avoid oversaturated color.
- Camera movement should be motivated by character action or story information.

## Reference-board graphic palette
The board uses burgundy/charcoal frames, cream information panels, and gold accents. Treat this as a design-board palette; scene grading may vary by location and dramatic beat while preserving the series identity.

## Image continuity
- Use approved character reference images as identity anchors.
- Preserve facial structure, hair, age impression, body proportions, and wardrobe.
- Keep location geography, lighting direction, and screen direction consistent.
- Do not use a collage as the sole image-to-video reference when a clean, individual character reference is available.

## Technical choices to lock
Aspect ratio, resolution, frame rate, lens language, grain, grading LUT, and platform delivery specs remain **TO CONFIRM** before production.
