# Continuity Bible — ميراث الدم

**Version:** v0.1  
**Status:** Mandatory production rules.

## Character continuity
- Approved reference is canonical for identity.
- No unmotivated face, age, hair, facial-hair, body, or voice changes.
- Wardrobe and accessories remain unchanged within a continuous scene unless scripted.
- Track injuries, dirt, sweat, tears, and emotional state across cuts.

## Screen and spatial continuity
- Maintain the established 180-degree axis and screen direction.
- Preserve eyelines, relative positions, entrances/exits, and travel direction.
- Do not reverse location geography between shots.
- Track time of day, weather, practical lights, and background activity.

## Prop continuity
Track each story-critical prop’s owner, hand, position, orientation, state, and transfer between shots. If the prop is not visible, its last known state still carries forward.

## Scene handoff
Every scene package must include a continuity-in and continuity-out note. Record unresolved mismatches in the continuity log; do not silently accept them.
