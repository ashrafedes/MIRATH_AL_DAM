# Dialogue & Voice Bible — ميراث الدم

**Version:** v0.1  
**Status:** Rules established; individual voice profiles to confirm.

## Dialogue
- Default dialogue is natural Egyptian colloquial Arabic.
- Use Modern Standard Arabic only when justified by character, setting, or document (e.g., formal legal language).
- Avoid exposition that characters already know.
- Give each character a distinct vocabulary, rhythm, and level of directness.
- Dialogue should carry subtext; avoid explaining the emotion already visible in performance.

## Voice continuity
For each character, record perceived age, pitch range, texture, pace, accent, breathiness/roughness, articulation, emotional range, and prohibited deviations. Use the same approved voice reference across scenes.

## Recording/generation rules
- Keep one clear speaker per dialogue shot whenever practical.
- Do not assign dialogue to a silent character.
- For lip-sync tests, use short lines, clear face visibility, minimal head turns, and one speaker at a time.
- Verify lip-sync and pronunciation; never assume generated dialogue is correct.
- Store approved dialogue text and voice takes with versioned IDs.
