## Required lock sheet per location
- Exact canonical name and ID
- City/neighborhood and period
- Floor plan, fixed orientation, entrances/exits, windows, and walkable paths
- Named zones and fixed furniture/set dressing
- Camera positions and 180-degree axis
- Lighting states by time of day
- Palette, materials, weather, and ambient sound
- Approved wide, medium, and detail references
- What may change between scenes and what must never change

## Continuity rule
Do not reverse the layout, move permanent architecture, or change the position of a continuity anchor between shots unless the script explicitly establishes the change.
