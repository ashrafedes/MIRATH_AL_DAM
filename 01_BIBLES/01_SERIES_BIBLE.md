# Series Bible — ميراث الدم

**Version:** v0.1  
**Status:** Working foundation; story canon incomplete  
**Format:** Serialized Egyptian drama  
**Primary language:** Egyptian Arabic  
**Visual direction:** Cinematic realism; dark burgundy, charcoal, warm cream, and restrained gold accents.

## Core premise
A family-centered drama built around inheritance, concealed history, competing loyalties, and the consequences of uncovering the truth. The precise central crime/mystery, causal chain, and season resolution must be taken from the owner-approved story version; they are **TO CONFIRM** here rather than invented.

## Dramatic engine
Each episode should advance at least two of the following:
- A new piece of evidence or a contradiction in the family’s account.
- A shift in allegiance, trust, or power.
- A choice that makes the inheritance conflict more personal.
- A consequence that carries into the next episode.

## Tone
Grounded Egyptian social drama with suspense and emotional realism. Avoid melodramatic exposition, arbitrary twists, and characters acting on information they could not know.

## Audience promise
The audience should feel that family history is being uncovered through concrete evidence, personal choices, and escalating consequences—not through unexplained revelations.

## Canon items to confirm from the locked story
- Final logline and central dramatic question
- Exact nature of the inheritance and legal dispute
- Inciting incident and timeline
- Identity, motive, and method of the central wrongdoing
- Episode count, episode titles, midpoint, climax, and ending
- Exact family relationships and any secrets
- Which character facts in the reference-board text are final story canon

No bible or scene package may invent a major plot fact to fill a gap. Label missing information **TO CONFIRM** and resolve it against the locked script.
