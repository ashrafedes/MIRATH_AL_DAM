# Production Workflow

1. **Script lock:** approve story, episode outline, script, and stable IDs.
2. **Design:** create character, location, and prop sheets; approve and register references.
3. **Scene package/storyboard:** include script excerpt, dramatic purpose, time/location, cast/wardrobe, props/states, blocking, eyelines, camera plan, dialogue/sound, prompts, and continuity bridges.
4. **Image-to-video:** use approved references; track model/settings and candidates. Reject identity, costume, prop, geography, anatomy, or unintended-motion drift.
5. **Voice, sound, edit:** maintain voice, room tone, scene geography, and screen direction.
6. **QC/delivery:** verify story, identity, wardrobe, props, eyelines, axis, audio sync, and technical specs.

A scene package must be executable without searching old chats or guessing missing details.
