## Production use
- Use the relevant character sheet as the identity reference for every appearance.
- Match face shape, eyes, nose, mouth, hair, facial hair, apparent age, body proportions, and distinctive details.
- Match wardrobe by scene and the exact outfit shown in the sheet.
- Do not mix clothing across outfit variants without a script-based costume change.
- Use close facial references for identity and full-body outfit panels for wardrobe.
- Record the character ID and reference filename in every scene and shot package.

## CHAR_001 — Hussein Al-Shenawy
- Name: حسين الشناوي
- Reference age: 70
- Role from sheet: Founder and head of the El-Shennawy family/business empire (cotton and real estate).
- Face/hair: Elderly Egyptian man; swept-back silver-white hair; thick grey-white moustache; deep-set dark eyes; lined face; stern, controlled expression.
- Wardrobe anchors: Formal dark navy suit, white shirt, burgundy patterned tie and pocket square; dark office suit; patterned dark home robe; dark casual home cardigan; pale sick-room robe/blanket.
- Signature details: Gold signet ring, gold wristwatch, dark cane, glasses as a prop.
- Performance range: Neutral authority, thoughtful concern, anger, sadness, pain, physical weakness.
- Continuity: Keep facial age, hair, moustache, ring/watch, and outfit per scene fixed. The sick look is a separate state and must not leak into healthy scenes.

## CHAR_002 — Layla Al-Shenawy
- Name: ليلى الشناوي
- Reference age: 29
- Role from sheet: Doctor (باطنة); granddaughter of Hajj Hussein Al-Shenawy and daughter of the late Maryam.
- Face/hair: Young Egyptian woman; olive/light-medium skin; expressive brown eyes; dark wavy hair, generally pinned loosely back with strands framing face; defined brows; small gold hoop earrings.
- Wardrobe anchors: White medical coat over dark navy scrubs with stethoscope and ID; burgundy formal birthday dress; beige trench coat over light top and jeans; dark investigation jacket; black mourning outfit.
- Signature details: Small gold watch, stethoscope, medical ID badge, brown leather bag.
- Performance range: Concerned, determined, sad, shocked, angry, calm/reassuring.
- Continuity: Preserve the same facial identity, hairline, eye shape, and earrings. Keep medical accessories only in the medical look unless scripted.
